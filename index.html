About Us

About 63 HR Solutions

We are a team of Human Resources and administrative specialists dedicated to helping businesses build organized, efficient and professional workplaces.

Instead of building a full HR department from day one, businesses can rely on 63 HR Solutions as an extension of their own team. We handle the HR and administrative work that keeps a company running smoothly, so management can focus on the business.

  • An extension of your team

    We work alongside your management as your HR and admin function.

  • HR and administration together

    People processes and office operations handled by one team.

  • Support that grows with you

    Start with what you need today and add services as you grow.
